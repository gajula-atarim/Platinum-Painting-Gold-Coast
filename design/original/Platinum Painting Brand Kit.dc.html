The Brand

Positioning

Platinum Painting is a Gold Coast painting company for homes, businesses and strata properties. We treat every coat as protection first: the right preparation and the right product so surfaces hold up against sun, salt air and storms.

Tagline

The Platinum Standard In Painting

Key message

Painting is about more than looks. It is a protective coating that helps surfaces last longer in all weather conditions.

Protection Every job starts with what the surface needs to last, then how it should look.
Precision Thorough prep, clean lines and a tidy site at the end of each day.
Local knowledge Products and systems chosen for coastal Queensland conditions.
Straight talk Clear quotes, honest timelines and advice you can act on.

Tone of Voice

Knowledgeable, calm and practical. We sound like an experienced tradesperson explaining the job: plain words, specific details, no hype.

We say "We pressure-clean, repair and prime before any topcoat goes on." "This membrane is rated for coastal UV and helps stop water getting in." "Your quote lists every product and step, so you know what you're paying for."
We avoid "Transform your home with stunning, world-class results!" "The cheapest painters on the Coast." Vague promises without products, prep or timeframes.
08

Photography

Real Platinum Painting jobs only, shot in natural daylight. Show the finished result, the preparation behind it, and the crew at work. No stock photos of people holding rollers.

The finished home Wide, straight-on, blue sky where possible.
The detail Crisp cut-in lines, render texture, roof membrane.
The preparation Cleaning, sanding, priming. Proof of the protection story.
